JSTOUHOU

A Touhou style danmaku shooter that runs in your browser

Size

Controls

←↑↓→Move, pick in menus
ZShoot, OK
XBomb, back
ShiftFocus: move slowly and show your hitbox
Esc PPause
FFull screen

How to play

  • Only the small dot in the middle of your character can be hit. Hold Shift to see it.
  • Bullets that pass close by count as graze and give points.
  • Bomb just after getting hit to save your life.
  • Options, Practice and saved Replays are on the title screen.
